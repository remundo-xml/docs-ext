# Absences

Request and manage time off.

## Route

`/absences`

## Features

- Request annual leave, sick leave, unpaid leave, parental leave, and other absence types
- View remaining entitlement (days available, used, and pending)
- Track approval status (Pending, Approved, Rejected, Overdue)
- Attach supporting documents (e.g. medical certificates)
- Delete pending or future requests

## How to request time off

### Step 1: Check your entitlement

Navigate to the **Absences** page from the sidebar. At the top you can see your entitlement summary: total days entitled, days used, days pending approval, and days remaining.

### Step 2: Create a new request

Click **Submit Absence Request**. Fill in the form:

- **Absence Type** — select from the dropdown (Annual Leave, Sick Leave, Unpaid Leave, Parental Leave, etc.). Available types depend on your contract.
- **Duration Type** — choose All Day or Half Day. If Half Day, select Morning or Afternoon.
- **Start Date** and **End Date** — select the date range. Weekends and public holidays are not counted towards your entitlement.
- **Note** (optional) — add context for your request.
- **File attachment** (optional) — upload a supporting document such as a medical certificate (PDF, JPG, PNG).

### Step 3: Review and submit

The system validates your request against your remaining entitlement and checks for conflicts with submitted timesheets or existing absences. If there are timesheet conflicts, you'll be asked whether to cancel those timesheets.

Click **Submit**, review the summary in the confirmation modal, and click **Confirm**.

## Managing absence requests

Your requests appear as cards showing the absence type, dates, number of days, and status:

- **Pending** (yellow hourglass) — awaiting manager approval. Can be deleted.
- **Approved** (green check) — confirmed. Can be deleted if the dates are still in the future.
- **Rejected** (red exclamation) — declined, with a rejection note. Can be deleted and resubmitted.
- **Overdue** (grey clock) — approval delayed beyond the expected timeframe. Can be deleted.
